Before you beg your parents for something good,
Ask yourself: "Did I deserve it?
Was I an obedient, sweet boy?"
If yes, ask twice as much.
If no, ask twice as pitifully.

If you've decided to go swimming
And jumped off a cliff.
But changed your mind mid-flight
To dive into a wet river,
Stop falling into the water
And fly back up.
Anyone can change their mind.

If you hit yourself with a hammer
on your finger - bam!
Don't blame the one who invented nails
for our misfortune,
Because undoubtedly,
The one to blame for your misfortune
is not the inventor of nails,
but the creator of the hammer.

If the tea in the glass is too long
It refuses to cool down
And you no longer have the patience
to sit and blow on it,
Tip your glass of hot tea
On your lap.
On scalded knees,
The tea gets cold very quickly.

If grandma is tired
And has sat down to rest,
Ring a couple of pot lids over her loudly.
A dozing old lady
Should be awakened in time -
Granny will immediately awaken to
a wealth of new, fresh energy.

If you're racing down the hallway
Rushing along on your bike,
And Dad comes out of the bathroom for a walk,
Don't turn into the kitchen,
There's a solid refrigerator in the kitchen.
Brake like Dad.
Dad is gentle. He will forgive.

A lost child
Must remember that he
Will be taken home as soon as
He gives his address.
You must act smarter,
Say: "I live
Near a palm tree with a monkey
On distant islands."
A lost child,
If he's not a fool,
Will not miss a chance
To visit different countries.

To drive out the various flies and mosquitoes from the apartment,
You have to pull down the curtain
And swing it over your head.
Pictures will fly off the walls,
Flowers from the windowsill.
The television will tumble,
The chandelier will crash into the parquet.
And, escaping from the roar,
The mosquitoes will fly away,
And the frightened flies
Flock to the south.
